Conventional gardens mimic immature ecosystems. They are usually dominated by early-succession plants. Most grasses, flowers, and especially annual vegetables are pioneers. This means that in our love of lawns and orderly gardens, we're attempting to keep our yards at an early stage of ecological development.

Weeds in our lawn and maple seedlings in the flower beds are testimony to succession's power. Viewed ecologically, the standard suburban yard just wants to grow up. Understanding this lets us ally ourselves with nature's considerable might, instead of battling her.
